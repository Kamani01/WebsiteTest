Coverage · WF7 and nearby

Featherstone first. Then the towns around it.

We pick up from home, college or work. If your postcode is on the round, you will not be asked to travel to a meeting point.

Local roads

Learn the roads you will drive on test.

Featherstone is a proper mix: quiet terraces for the first hours, the A645 through town, roundabouts toward the M62, and the faster runs into Pontefract and Wakefield.

That mix is useful. Beginners get space. Test-ready learners get traffic, lanes, and independent driving without a motorway leap they are not ready for.

Towns

Where we pick up.

Base

Featherstone & Purston

Station Lane, the high street, residential grids, and the A645. This is where most first lessons start.

Test prep

Pontefract

Nearest practical test centre for many WF7 learners. We use the approach roads, town-centre junctions and independent-driving stretches you will meet on the day.

Wider round

Castleford, Ackworth, Wakefield

Pickup is available across these towns subject to the diary. Ask with your postcode when you book.
