Clockwise routes

Your roundabout. Left-hand traffic. Blue, green and orange go first once the north car holds the east traffic on the red line.

Left-hand traffic, clockwise only. All three cars enter from the south: blue exits west, green exits north, orange goes the long way round and exits east. Blue signals left from the start. Green signals left as it passes the first exit. Orange stays inner past the first exit, then signals left at the second exit and drifts to the outer lane before leaving at the third. The grey cars sit in three lanes and signal too. A car from the north holds the grey east traffic at its red line, so blue, green and orange, which have driven up to the line, go first. 14-second loop.
