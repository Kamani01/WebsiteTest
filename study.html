Clockwise routes, split by colour

Left-hand traffic, clockwise only. All three enter from the south. Blue leaves west, green leaves north, orange goes the long way round and leaves east.

Blue · first exit, west Left lane. Left signal on from the start. Leave at the first exit.
Green · second exit, north Middle lane. Left signal as you pass the first exit. Leave straight ahead.
Orange · third exit, east Right lane. Stay inner past the first exit. Left signal at the second exit, drift to the outer lane, leave at the third.

Ten-second loop. One second on the south line, then they move off together. The amber lamp is the left signal. Cars stay the same size and face the way they are travelling.
