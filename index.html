Questions

Before you sit in the car.

Do I need a provisional licence?

Yes. Bring it to the first lesson. We will also do the eyesight check the examiner uses on test day.

Manual or automatic — which should I choose?

If you are happy to learn gears, manual keeps more cars open after you pass. If clutch work is slowing you down or you already know you want an automatic, we will teach that from the start. You cannot take a manual test on an automatic licence.

How many hours will I need?

The DVSA still finds most learners need around 45 hours of professional tuition plus private practice. Some need fewer, some more. We would rather tell you honestly after two or three lessons than guess on a website.

Can you take me to test?

Yes, when you are ready. We prepare on local routes and can provide the dual-control car on the day. Test fees are booked and paid through GOV.UK.
