What is included in a lesson?

One-to-one tuition in a dual-control car, planned around the DVSA syllabus. Pickup from home, college or work if your postcode is on the round.

How much are lessons?

Manual from £36 an hour, or £340 for 10 hours. Automatic from £38, or £360 for 10 hours. Intensive is quoted after a £70 assessment. DVSA test fees are paid to the DVSA.

Do you cover my area?

Featherstone first, then Wolverhampton, Shareshill, Essington, Coven, Cheslyn Hay, Bushbury, Fallings Park, Hilton and Brinsford. Ask with your postcode.

Can I prepare for the Cat & Kittens Lane test?

Yes. We practise the approach, roundabouts and independent-driving stretches used at the DVSA centre on Cat & Kittens Lane, WV10 7JD.

Does sending a request confirm a lesson?

No. We'll reply with a slot, usually the same day. A lesson is confirmed only when we agree a time.
