Privacy Policy

How JS Driving School uses the details you send us.

What we collect

When you send a lesson request we collect your name, phone number, pickup postcode, lesson type, any preferred times, and optionally your email and message.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your request, offer lesson slots, and arrange lessons. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to deal with your request and any lessons that follow, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time. Call 07447 432160 and we will deal with it.

Google reviews

Reviews are left and shown on Google. We link to them and do not copy or edit them.
