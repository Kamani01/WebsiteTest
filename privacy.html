Privacy

JS Driving School collects only what we need to book and deliver lessons: your name, phone number, postcode, and any notes you send. We do not sell data. Booking details are used to arrange tuition and, if you ask, to stay in touch about lesson times.

The booking form on this site opens WhatsApp with the details you type. It does not store them on the website. This preview does not use advertising cookies. When a domain and booking email are connected, this notice can be updated with a contact address for data requests.
